🐾 Delete your account

DesktopPetMessenger, the pet messenger for two.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the chat and tap Options (the gear).
  2. Open the 👤 Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account…, type DELETE and confirm.

It's deleted right away.

Without the app

Lost your phone, or can't sign in? Email ancestralsoftworks@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my account.

We delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and answer when it's done. We only act on emails sent from the account's own address, so nobody can delete someone else's.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and password), your profile and your pet, with its stars and accessories.
  • Your chat, for both people in it: every message, photo, video, file and voice message, the custom emojis and backgrounds. A chat belongs to both of you, so it can't stay half there.
  • Your devices and their keys, your spare key backup, your notification tokens and any feedback you sent.

Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we could never read them anyway. Our hosting provider keeps automatic database backups for a limited time; a deleted account disappears from those when they expire. A spare key file you saved stays wherever you saved it: delete it yourself.
